Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Renzo ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that data.

What We Collect

When you use Renzo, we collect the following:

  • Account information — your name and email address when you sign up.
  • Contact data you import — names, companies, email addresses, notes, and other fields from CSV files you upload. This data is yours; we store it only to provide the service.
  • Message history — outreach messages you generate or log through Renzo, stored so the AI can write contextually relevant follow-ups.
  • Usage data — basic activity logs (e.g., when outreach was sent) used to power the dashboard and contact scoring.

We do not collect any data passively through tracking pixels, third-party analytics, or advertising networks.

How It Is Stored

All data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted on Supabase, a managed database platform with servers located in the United States. Data is encrypted at rest and in transit. We use row-level security so your data is logically isolated from other users.

How We Use Your Data

We use your data exclusively to provide the Renzo service:

  • Generating AI-powered outreach messages personalized to your contacts and history.
  • Calculating contact priority scores and outreach schedules.
  • Displaying your activity log and dashboard statistics.

We never sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing or any other purpose. We do not use your contact data or message content to train AI models.

Credit Card Data

Payments are processed by Stripe, a PCI-compliant payment processor. Renzo never sees, stores, or has access to your full credit card number. All payment data is handled exclusively by Stripe under their own privacy and security policies.

Cookies and Tracking

Renzo does not use cookies beyond what is strictly necessary to maintain your login session (a short-lived token stored in browser session storage). We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking, or third-party analytics.

Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing davidlgenuth@gmail.com. We will delete your data within 30 days of receiving your request. Note that contact data you imported belongs to you — we retain no copies after deletion.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of Renzo after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at davidlgenuth@gmail.com.
