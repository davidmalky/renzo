Any System (CSV Export)
Live
Universal — works with everything

Export contacts from any CRM, ERP, or spreadsheet as a CSV file and import directly into Renzo. Works with Salesforce, HubSpot, QuickBooks, Excel, Google Sheets — anything.

How to connect:
  1. Open your CRM or spreadsheet
  2. Export contacts to CSV (File → Export → CSV)
  3. Go to Contacts → Import CSV in Renzo
  4. Renzo auto-detects your columns and imports
✉
Email (Gmail / Outlook)
Live
Send outreach from your inbox

Renzo opens email drafts directly in Gmail or Outlook web. Your messages go out from your own email — your signature, your sent folder, your reply-to.

Setup:
  1. Go to Settings → Email Accounts
  2. Add your email address with a label and pick a provider (Gmail or Outlook)
  3. Renzo opens that provider's web compose for every send -- works with custom domains too (Google Workspace, Microsoft 365)
  4. Your sent folder, signature, and reply-to are all your own
⚙
Custom System / VCS
Live
Your own apps and databases

If you have a custom app like the Vendor Catalog Scheduler (VCS), you can embed Renzo directly or export data for import.

Two ways to integrate:
Option 1 — CSV Export: Add an export button to your system that downloads contacts as CSV. Import into Renzo.

Option 2 — Embed Renzo: Renzo can be embedded as an iframe or panel inside your custom app. Add this to any HTML page:
<iframe src="https://renzo-beige.vercel.app" width="100%" height="700" frameborder="0"></iframe>

Salesforce
Coming Soon
CRM — Contacts, Accounts, Activities

Direct Salesforce sync is on the roadmap. Today you can use the CSV export path below, or join the waitlist to be notified when the native connector ships.

Use CSV in the meantime:
  1. In Salesforce: Reports → New Report → Contacts & Accounts
  2. Add columns: Name, Account Name, Email, Phone, Last Activity Date, Contract End Date, Account Revenue
  3. Run report → Export → CSV
  4. Import the CSV into Renzo

CRM — Contacts, Deals, Companies

A native HubSpot connector is in development. Join the waitlist and we'll email you when it goes live.

Use CSV in the meantime:
  1. HubSpot → Contacts → All Contacts
  2. Click Actions → Export
  3. Select: Email, Company, Last Contacted, Deal Stage, Phone
  4. Download CSV → Import into Renzo
